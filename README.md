## Featured Project
### Mission X Sales Trends Dashboard
#### Project Overview
Developed a multi-page Power BI analytics solution as part of the Mission Ready Data Analyst Accelerator.

The solution combines sales reporting, time-series analysis, customer insights, product performance evaluation, forecasting, and industry benchmarking to support data-driven decision-making.

#### Skills Demonstrated
- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Modelling
- KPI Development
- Data Visualisation
- Forecasting
- Customer Analytics
- Product Analytics
- Business Intelligence Reporting

### Solution Components
#### Sales Trends Overview
Analysis of daily, monthly and weekday sales patterns to identify trends and fluctuations in performance.

<img width="1602" height="947" alt="Sales Trend Overview" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6d832da4-3bba-422b-8612-b31f30a275f6" />

#### Country Performance Dashboard
Country-level sales analysis, country group comparison, and top-performing country evaluation.

#### Time-Based Sales Performance Analysis
Year-to-date and month-on-month growth analysis using time-intelligence measures and variance reporting.

#### Product Performance Dashboard
Product-category performance analysis, product contribution assessment, and categorized versus uncategorized product evaluation.

#### Customer Insights Dashboard
Customer segmentation, spending behaviour analysis, customer activity patterns, and geographic customer distribution.

#### Forecasting
Forecast modelling and outlier analysis to evaluate future sales performance scenarios and prediction accuracy.

<img width="1592" height="950" alt="Forecasting" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/5dc8d7a7-5dc9-4f6c-8cd2-cd3fcad9d3f6" />

#### Industry Benchmarking
Comparison of sales metrics against industry-level e-commerce performance indicators and spending patterns.

#### Data Model
The solution was developed using a multi-table data model incorporating sales, product, invoice, date, and secondary benchmark datasets connected through established table relationships and DAX measures.
